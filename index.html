Curatorial Statement

This digital exhibit features Spoliarium by Juan Luna, a powerful artwork that reflects themes of struggle, loss, and human dignity. The piece was carefully selected because of its historical importance and its ability to communicate deep emotions through visual storytelling.

In this exhibit, we aim to present not only the artistic beauty of Spoliarium but also its deeper meaning and relevance. By exploring this work, viewers are encouraged to reflect on history, human experience, and how art can express powerful messages beyond what is seen.
